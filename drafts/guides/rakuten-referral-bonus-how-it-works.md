## What the current $50 offer says

Rakuten's standing Program Terms do not lock in a fixed dollar amount. They send you to the Referral Site for the Referral Bonus Terms, which "may change from time to time."

On Nov 13, 2026, that official Referral Site advertised a $50 Referral Bonus. The legal footnote on the same page says you qualify for a $50 Referral Bonus when a person you refer:

- signs up as a new member using your referral link (or another method Rakuten supplies) during the Offer Period, and
- makes Qualifying Purchase(s) totaling at least $50 within 90 days of signing up.

The Offer Period on that page is 12:00 AM PT October 1, 2026 to 11:59 PM PT December 31, 2026. The same footnote says the offer may be modified or cancelled anytime, and that you may qualify for a Referral Bonus more than one time. The page also uses the marketing line "Bonuses are unlimited, so keep on referring."

For this specific $50 offer, the footnote requires you and your referee to reside and be located in the 50 United States or District of Columbia. That is narrower than the standing Program Terms, which still describe the overall program as open to members in the 50 United States, District of Columbia, or Canada. Do not assume a Canada-based signup qualifies for this $50 promotion.

The Referral Bonus is added to your next Cash Back payment, and only if the referee is a Qualified Referral under the Program Terms.

**Checked Nov 13, 2026.** Source: [Rakuten Referral Site](https://www.rakuten.com/referral/default.do) and [Rakuten Referral Program Terms](https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referral-program-terms) (updated 10/8/2024).

## Does the new member also get $50?

The standing Program Terms say a referee may qualify for a Referral Sign Up Bonus, that those terms also live on the Referral Site and may change, and that there is one Referral Sign Up Bonus per eligible new member.

The official Referral Site, checked Nov 13, 2026, markets the current promotion as "Give and get unlimited $50 bonuses." The same page's social description tells a new member, "you'll get $50 after you spend $50." The legal footnote on that page spells out the referrer's $50 Referral Bonus and the $50 qualifying spend. It does not, in that footnote, separately define a $50 Sign Up Bonus for the new member.

Treat the new-member amount as whatever the official Referral Site or signup flow shows on the day the friend joins. The official FAQ says bonus amounts can change, and "the person who was referred to join will get the bonus that was available on day of sign up."

**Checked Nov 13, 2026.** Sources: [Referral Program Terms, section 3.2](https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referral-program-terms), [Referral Site](https://www.rakuten.com/referral/default.do), and [Refer-A-Friend Program FAQ](https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/refer-a-friend-program-faq-360002117087).

## What makes a referral qualified

Under the Program Terms, a Qualified Referral is a referee who must:

- be a natural person (not a business, legal entity, or pet) residing in and located in the 50 United States, District of Columbia, or Canada (again, the current $50 offer footnote tightens this to the 50 United States or District of Columbia);
- be a new member on rakuten.com;
- sign up using the referrer's custom referral link, or another method Rakuten supplies that allows proper tracking;
- make purchase(s) that earn Cash Back through rakuten.com, the Rakuten App, or the Rakuten browser extension within 90 days of signup, according to the applicable bonus terms;
- consent to and comply with Rakuten's Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy;
- be the person who pays for the Qualifying Purchase.

Purchases made on Amazon.com are not Qualifying Purchases, except purchases on [music.amazon.com](https://music.amazon.com/). The current offer footnote also says a referee is not a Qualified Referral if they cancel, return, or exchange the Qualifying Purchase, if the purchase has not earned Cash Back, or if the purchase is found to be fraudulent, abusive, unethical, suspicious, or otherwise inconsistent with the Program Terms.

Self-referral and making purchases on behalf of someone else, or under a Rakuten account that is not yours, are not allowed.

**Checked Nov 13, 2026.** Source: [Referral Program Terms, sections 2 and 7](https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referral-program-terms) and the offer footnote on the [Referral Site](https://www.rakuten.com/referral/default.do).

## When the bonus is confirmed and paid

Two official pages describe timing, and they are not identical, so both are quoted here.

The Referral Site FAQ says: "Generally, you'll receive your referral bonus within 60 days of your referral's qualifying purchase." The same page defines pending as a friend who has joined but may not have a verified qualifying purchase yet, and confirmed as a friend who has joined, made a qualifying purchase, and had the details confirmed.

Rakuten's official payment help, updated October 30, 2026, says referral bonuses "will be confirmed once your friend makes a qualifying purchase and their Cash Back is confirmed." It also says:

- Online and in-store Cash Back typically takes 3 to 14 weeks to be confirmed, in case of returns, exchanges, exclusions, or other order changes.
- Dining Cash Back is confirmed within 3 to 5 days after you dine.
- Travel Cash Back is typically confirmed within 45 to 120 days after you complete the booking.
- Bonuses depend on a qualifying order and are confirmed at the same time as the related Cash Back.
- Confirmed Cash Back, bonuses, and other rewards are paid by the method you selected every three months, on or by February 15, May 15, August 15, and November 15.
- You need $5 or more in confirmed Cash Back to get paid. A confirmed balance under $5 carries over to the next payment date.
- Payment methods listed on that page are PayPal, Bilt Points, Samsung Cash, or check. Eligible American Express Card Members can choose Membership Rewards points instead of Cash Back. You can also redeem a confirmed balance of at least $5 for a gift card, subject to certain limits, and Rakuten says that can arrive within minutes.

Because the referral bonus waits for the friend's Cash Back to be confirmed, a purchase that is still pending does not yet lock in the bonus. Returns and cancelled orders can remove it.

**Checked Nov 13, 2026.** Sources: [Referral Site FAQ](https://www.rakuten.com/referral/default.do) and [When Will I Get Paid?](https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/when-will-i-get-paid).

## Where you may (and may not) share a Rakuten link

Rakuten allows you to post your referral link on a page you own and control. A BonusBridge page that we operate qualifies as that kind of page. The Program Terms do not allow posting the link on a page you do not own or control, including merchant, Facebook, or forum pages.

The same restrictions list also prohibits: self-referral; fake accounts or sites; bulk email or other spam to strangers; bidding on keywords containing "Ebates" or "Rakuten" (including common misspellings); putting Rakuten trademarks in ad text, extensions, or banner ads; and paid advertising that sends traffic directly to your referral link.

If you want a broader commercial arrangement, Rakuten's Program Terms point interested people to a Sponsored Influencer application. That is separate from the member Refer-A-Friend link on this site.

**Checked Nov 13, 2026.** Source: [Referral Program Terms, sections 5 and 7](https://www.rakuten.com/help/article/referral-program-terms).

## Questions people ask

### Is the Rakuten referral bonus always $50?

No. The Program Terms say Referral Bonus Terms live on the Referral Site and may change from time to time. The $50 figure, the $50 qualifying spend, and the October 1 to December 31, 2026 Offer Period were on the official Referral Site on Nov 13, 2026. Confirm that page before you treat $50 as still current.

### Do Amazon.com purchases count toward the referral bonus?

No, with one official exception. The Program Terms say purchases on Amazon.com are not Qualifying Purchases, other than purchases on music.amazon.com.

### How long does the new member have to qualify?

The Program Terms and the current offer footnote both use a 90-day window from signup. For the current $50 offer, Qualifying Purchase(s) must total at least $50 in that window, earn Cash Back, and not be cancelled, returned, or exchanged.

### When does the referrer actually receive the $50?

The Referral Site says you will generally receive the referral bonus within 60 days of the qualifying purchase. Official payment help says the bonus is confirmed only after the friend's qualifying purchase and their Cash Back are confirmed, and that online and in-store Cash Back typically takes 3 to 14 weeks to confirm. The bonus is then added to your next Cash Back payment. You need $5 or more in confirmed Cash Back to be paid on a quarterly payment date, unless you redeem an eligible confirmed balance for a gift card.

### Can I post my Rakuten referral link in a Facebook group or on a forum?

No. The Program Terms prohibit posting your referral link on any page that is not owned and controlled by you, including merchant, Facebook, or forum pages. They also prohibit paid ads that send traffic directly to the referral link. The link on BonusBridge is on a site we operate.

## Who gets the $10, and who does not

The current promotion on TopCashback's official referral terms, last updated 08/15/2026 and checked Nov 13, 2026, pays the referrer $10 Cash Back for each qualifying referral. The $10 is not a signup gift to the new member.

The same terms do not state a fixed referral bonus for the person who joins. Any welcome offer the new member sees is whatever TopCashback is advertising at signup, and it is subject to change. The terms also say the bonus you (the referrer) receive is the amount advertised on the site at the time your friend signed up through your link. That amount can differ from what was shown when you sent the link, or when the bonus was later credited.

The official Refer a Friend page uses the headline "Refer a friend & earn $10" and the steps "They sign up and earn at least $10 Cash Back" and "You get a bonus." The legal terms are more precise than that marketing copy: the $10 the new member must earn has to be eligible, available Cash Back from qualifying retailers.

**Checked Nov 13, 2026.** Sources: [Refer a Friend Terms](https://www.topcashback.com/referrals/terms/) and [Refer a Friend page](https://www.topcashback.com/refer-a-friend/).

## The requirements, step by step

You receive a referral bonus only if all of the following are true:

1. Your friend clicks your unique Refer a Friend link and signs up for TopCashback through that link.
2. Your friend verifies their TopCashback account.
3. Your friend meets the Cash Back requirement in the current promotion: a minimum of $10 in eligible Cash Back from qualifying retailers within 365 days of joining.
4. That Cash Back has become available. The terms say: "Once the new member has $10 available Cash Back, the referrer will receive the applicable referral bonus." They also say the referral bonus "will become payable when your referred friend's Cash Back is available."

The official Refer a Friend page says the same thing in shorter form: once the person you referred has earned the required Cash Back amount and it has become payable, your bonus will be available to cash out.

Pending, in-progress, or excluded Cash Back does not meet the $10 available threshold. An unverified account cannot qualify. Signing up later through the homepage, without the unique link, does not meet the click-and-join rule in the terms.

The program is open to TopCashback USA members in good standing (the account has not been restricted or deactivated). Self-referral is not allowed. Referral bonuses are paid only for genuine signups, and they are not paid if the new account has been terminated.

**Checked Nov 13, 2026.** Sources: [Refer a Friend Terms, Current Promotion and sections 2, 3, and 4](https://www.topcashback.com/referrals/terms/) and [Refer a Friend page](https://www.topcashback.com/refer-a-friend/).

## What does not count toward the $10

This is the part that most often causes a missed bonus. TopCashback's terms exclude all of the following from the new member's qualifying total:

- Cash Back awarded as goodwill payments, incentives, or contest winnings
- Additional sign-up bonuses
- Bonus promotions
- New member deals
- Cash Back awarded from retailers on the excluded list in section 8, using the list that was in force when the new member signed up

Section 8 is a long official list. On Nov 13, 2026 it included many "Freebie" and "Reward" offers, plus named retailers and categories such as Booking.com, IHG Hotels & Resorts, Skyscanner, ALL Accor, several VPN services, iStock, McAfee, Crutchfield, and "TopCashback - Sign-Up Bonus." The list can change. Use the live terms, not a copied list from this page, as the source of truth.

The terms also say you must not promote your referral link alongside any other new member incentives unless the promotion's terms expressly allow it. If more than one promotion is earned for the same signup, a bonus or incentive may be removed.

**Checked Nov 13, 2026.** Source: [Refer a Friend Terms, sections 4 and 8](https://www.topcashback.com/referrals/terms/).

## When the referrer can cash out, and how members get paid

The referral bonus becomes payable when the new member's qualifying Cash Back is available, not when a purchase is first tracked.

TopCashback's official help describes the ordinary Cash Back clock this way, checked Nov 13, 2026:

- After a tracked purchase, Cash Back is "In Progress."
- It is approved after the retailer reviews the transaction.
- It becomes "Available" only after the retailer has received an invoice from the affiliate network.
- Each retailer has a different time period. The Earnings page shows an estimate, and that estimate is not a guarantee.
- For a vacation purchase, you must complete the stay before Cash Back can move to Available.
- Once Cash Back is Available, you can withdraw it at any time.

Official help and site terms list these payout methods: bank transfer (ACH is only available to US bank account holders), Venmo, PayPal, and gift cards or e-gift cards. The receive-Cash-Back help page names Amazon, Starbucks, Target, and Sephora as examples of gift card options.

Those help pages do not state a minimum cash-out amount. This draft does not invent one. The $10 figure in the referral terms is the new member's eligible available Cash Back threshold for the referrer's bonus, not a documented withdrawal minimum.

**Checked Nov 13, 2026.** Sources: [Refer a Friend Terms, section 3](https://www.topcashback.com/referrals/terms/), [When will I get my Cash Back?](https://www.topcashback.com/help/cash-back-statuses/), [Why isn't my Cash Back available to payout yet?](https://www.topcashback.com/help/cash-back-not-payable/), [How do I receive my Cash Back?](https://www.topcashback.com/help/receive-cash-back/), [How will I get paid?](https://www.topcashback.com/help/earning-cash-back/), and [TopCashback Terms & Conditions](https://www.topcashback.com/terms/).

## A $5 cap that applies to comparison websites

The Refer a Friend terms describe a $10 referrer bonus for the current promotion. TopCashback's general site terms add a separate rule for commercial promotion.

Those general terms say that referral bonuses earned through commercial means, including comparison websites, "will earn no more than $5 per referral subject to minimum thresholds," unless express written consent is given and signed by TopCashback. They also tell you to read the Referral Terms before sending invites.

BonusBridge is a comparison website. Until an official affiliate agreement or other written consent is in place, we do not promise that a signup through this site will pay the site operator $10 rather than the commercial cap. A typical member who refers family and friends under the Refer a Friend terms is still described, in the current promotion, as eligible for $10 after the new member qualifies.

If you promote TopCashback to a wider audience, the referral terms also tell content creators to consider TopCashback's separate [affiliate program](https://www.topcashback.com/dyn/affiliate-program/). Paid search and other paid ads that use the referral link are not allowed without TopCashback's prior permission.

**Checked Nov 13, 2026.** Sources: [TopCashback Terms & Conditions](https://www.topcashback.com/terms/) and [Refer a Friend Terms, sections 6 and 7](https://www.topcashback.com/referrals/terms/).

## Questions people ask

### Do I get $10 for joining TopCashback through a referral link?

The current referral terms pay the $10 to the member who shared the link, after you qualify. They do not state a fixed $10 (or any other fixed amount) for the new member. Check TopCashback's signup page for any welcome offer running on the day you join. Welcome Cash Back, bonus promotions, and new member deals do not count toward the referrer's $10 threshold.

### How long do I have to reach $10 in Cash Back?

365 days from the day you join, and only eligible Cash Back from qualifying retailers counts. It also has to become available, not merely in progress.

### Does bonus Cash Back or a freebie offer count toward the $10?

No. The terms exclude goodwill, incentives, contest winnings, additional signup bonuses, bonus promotions, new member deals, and Cash Back from retailers on the excluded list in section 8.

### When does the referrer actually receive the bonus?

After the new member has $10 available eligible Cash Back. Official help says Cash Back becomes Available only after retailer approval and after the retailer has received an invoice from the affiliate network. Timing varies by retailer. Vacation Cash Back waits until the stay is completed. Once the referral bonus is payable, it can be cashed out with other Available Cash Back.

### Why do some pages mention $5 instead of $10?

The current Refer a Friend promotion states a $10 referrer bonus. TopCashback's general site terms separately cap referral bonuses earned through commercial means, including comparison websites, at $5 per referral unless TopCashback has given signed written consent. That cap is about commercial promotion, not the ordinary friend-to-friend promotion described in the referral terms.
